Our Story

Since 1903

Built in 1903 and listed on the National Register of Historic Places, our Victorian home sits on a wooded acre on the Historic Loop, a tree-lined walk from downtown Eureka Springs. Eleven rooms, suites and cottages share its porches, decks and garden gazebo.

Today innkeepers and chefs Kyle & Jesse, the Crossland family, welcome every guest as one of their own.

Kyle & Jesse

Breakfast

Award-winning gourmet breakfast, served at your own private table.

Coffee and tea from 8:00, then a leisurely breakfast at 9:00 in our sunlit dining room. Everything is made in-house from fresh, local ingredients, from Jesse’s Crème Brûlée French Toast to Baked Eggs Benedict with hollandaise, with Intelligentsia coffee.

“The Best Breakfast in the Ozarks” The New York Times

Weddings

Say ‘I do’ in our garden gazebo

Sweet & Simple Wedding Package – $349

A ceremony in our gazebo beneath the shade trees, above a wooded ravine (or in the lobby if you prefer). Includes a non-denominational minister, wedding music and photos taken on your own camera or phone. Up to six guests. Added to your stay in any room; overnight stay required.
